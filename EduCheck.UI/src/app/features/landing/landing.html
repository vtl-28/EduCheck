About EduCheck

EduCheck was created to solve a growing problem in South Africa's education space: the unchecked rise of unregistered private learning institutions. Thousands of students unknowingly enroll in programs that lead to invalid qualifications, wasting time, money, and opportunity. EduCheck steps in to provide transparency, trust, and tools to verify institutions before committing.
